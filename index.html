High Protein Zero Cholesterol

Moong Dal & Palak Chilla

15 Mins Prep • Breakfast Special

🛒 Key Ingredients:

Yellow Moong Dal (1 cup), Grated Paneer (50g), Chopped Palak (1/2 cup), Ginger, Green Chilli, Cumin, Turmeric.

👨‍🍳 Cooking Method:
  1. Soak moong dal overnight (or 3 hrs in warm water).
  2. Grind soaked dal with ginger & chilli into a smooth batter.
  3. Fold in chopped spinach, cumin, haldi, and salt.
  4. Pour onto a warm non-stick tava with 1/2 tsp oil. Cook until golden.
  5. Stuff with grated paneer and serve fresh with green chutney.

Overnight Chia & Nut Buster Oats

5 Mins Prep • No Cooking Required

🛒 Key Ingredients:

Rolled Oats (1/2 cup), Chia Seeds (1 tbsp), Chopped Walnuts/Almonds, Skim Milk, Apple, Cinnamon.

👨‍🍳 Cooking Method:
  1. In a jar or bowl, combine oats, chia seeds, and cinnamon powder.
  2. Pour in skim milk or almond milk and stir thoroughly.
  3. Cover and chill in the fridge overnight (at least 6 hours).
  4. In the morning, top with fresh diced apple & crushed walnuts. No sugar added!

Liver-Detox Lauki & Jeera Sabzi

20 Mins • Ideal Light Dinner

🛒 Key Ingredients:

Bottle Gourd/Lauki (1 medium), Tomatoes (2), Cumin (Jeera), Turmeric (Haldi), Fresh Coriander, 1 tsp Mustard Oil.

👨‍🍳 Cooking Method:
  1. Heat 1 tsp mustard oil in a pressure cooker. Splutter cumin seeds.
  2. Add chopped tomatoes, haldi, coriander powder, and salt. Cook till soft.
  3. Toss in diced lauki and 1/4 cup water.
  4. Pressure cook for 2-3 whistles. Garnish with heaps of fresh dhania.
  5. Pair with 1 plain multigrain roti for minimum liver workload.

Spiced Makhana & Chana Crunch

10 Mins • Perfect 4 PM Evening Snack

🛒 Key Ingredients:

Phool Makhana (2 cups), Roasted Chana (1 cup), Chaat Masala, Roasted Cumin, Black Salt, 1/2 tsp Ghee.

👨‍🍳 Cooking Method:
  1. Dry roast makhana in a pan over low heat for 5-7 mins until crispy.
  2. Add roasted chana into the pan.
  3. Drizzle 1/2 tsp ghee/olive oil and toss to coat evenly.
  4. Sprinkle chaat masala, jeera powder, and black salt. Store in an airtight container!

Grilled Protein & Quinoa Power Bowl

25 Mins • Power Lunch

🛒 Key Ingredients:

Quinoa / Brown Rice, Tofu or Chicken Breast (150g), Broccoli, Bell Peppers, Garlic, Olive Oil.

👨‍🍳 Cooking Method:
  1. Boil quinoa in 1 cup water until fluffy (12 mins).
  2. Sauté garlic and cubed tofu/chicken in 1 tsp olive oil until golden.
  3. Add broccoli and peppers; stir-fry on high heat for 3-4 mins (crunchy).
  4. Mix cooked quinoa with veggies & protein. Season with pepper & lemon juice.

Soya Chunks & Palak Detox Curry

20 Mins • Lunch / Dinner

🛒 Key Ingredients:

Soya Chunks (1 cup), Spinach Puree (2 cups), Chopped Onion, Tomato Puree, Ginger-Garlic Paste, Spices.

👨‍🍳 Cooking Method:
  1. Boil soya chunks in salted water for 5 mins; squeeze water out completely.
  2. Sauté chopped onions & ginger-garlic paste in 1 tsp oil until golden.
  3. Add tomato puree, haldi, & red chilli powder; cook till oil releases.
  4. Add fresh spinach puree and squeezed soya chunks. Simmer for 8 mins.

Steamed Beetroot & Mint Detox Salad

10 Mins • Side Salad

🛒 Key Ingredients:

Beetroot (1), Carrot (1), Cucumber (1), Mint Leaves, Lemon Juice, Black Pepper.

👨‍🍳 Cooking Method:
  1. Lightly steam diced beetroot and carrots for 5 minutes (keeps active enzymes intact).
  2. Let cool, then toss with fresh cucumber cubes & torn mint leaves.
  3. Dress with fresh lemon juice & black pepper right before eating.

Golden Turmeric & Ginger Night Brew

7 Mins • Pre-Bedtime Night Drink

🛒 Key Ingredients:

Skim Milk/Water (1 cup), Raw Haldi (1/2 tsp), Crushed Ginger, Pinch of Black Pepper, Cinnamon.

👨‍🍳 Cooking Method:
  1. Boil milk/water with crushed ginger, cinnamon, and haldi powder on low heat.
  2. Simmer for 5 minutes so bioactive compounds dissolve thoroughly.
  3. Add a tiny pinch of black pepper (boosts curcumin absorption by 2000%!).
  4. Strain and drink warm 30 mins before sleep to aid overnight liver cell repair.
